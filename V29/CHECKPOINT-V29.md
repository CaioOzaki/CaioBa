# Cloudcourt v29 — Refinement

Cloudcourt v29 adds guided practice, cooperative bot refinements, contact
feedback, match summaries, small character reactions and cosmetic skill
rewards. The startup splash now pairs the Cloudcourt wordmark with our emblem.
Extras appears on the first format selector and is hidden after choosing
2v2 or 4v4.

## Practice and play

Serve, receive, set and spike drills provide visible targets, precision
feedback, three difficulty settings and quick repeat. Practice runs offline
and keeps drill results separate from match statistics.

Bots use bounded ball-flight prediction to improve coverage and choose one
receiver. Cooperative Mine! and Set! captions use original musical cues;
they are not spoken voices. Character-specific small reactions supplement
the existing celebrations.

Accepted local contacts have distinct weak-contact, controlled-receive and
strong-attack sound and haptic feedback. The existing controller backend
and effect ownership paths remain in use.

## Match summaries

The summary shows aces, direct attack points, receptions, errors, contacts
and the longest rally, with Blue, Red and your own totals. A completed
best-of-three match is retained when returning to the menu. Partial match
statistics can be opened manually; an abandoned match does not replace the
last completed summary.

Direct attack points use a conservative proxy: a fast driven final contact
toward the opposing court, followed by an in-court point for that team.
Soft sets and service or touch faults do not count as direct attack points.
These summaries use accepted game events rather than a full volleyball
refereeing or professional scouting model.

## Skill rewards

Short skill goals unlock three sets of original outfits, faces and accents.
Unlocked categories can be selected independently.

| Set | Unlock |
| --- | --- |
| Sunset outfit, Wink face, gold accent | 5 accurate practice serves or 2 match aces |
| Skyline outfit, Focus face, sky accent | 10 controlled receptions and assisted sets combined |
| Emerald outfit, Starry Joy face, mint accent | 5 direct attack points or 5 accurate practice spikes |

Assisted sets count when a different teammate accepts the next contact.
Progress is saved locally. There are no purchases.

## Retained behavior and compatibility

The v28 Indoor and Beach block accounting, legal next contact by the blocker,
serve faults, Bomb court bounds and rosters, ball artwork and transparency,
seven hats, same-avatar customization preview, categorized Settings,
independent volume controls and original lofi music remain.

This is a PC VR modification for the separately owned compatible Volleyball
Fever Steam build 9216628. The package accepts the compatible original or
Cloudcourt-v28-extras-customization. Install v29 on every online peer.
Standard 2v2/4v4 and Bomb 2v2/4v4 use separate v29 room namespaces.

Close the game before running Instalar.cmd. Extract the entire ZIP first.
The installer verifies all 17 files and keeps a recovery backup.
Restaurar.cmd restores the compatible original. Existing release ZIPs
and the immutable v28 baseline are preserved.

## Verification and source

Controlled native fixtures cover the new feature paths and boundary cases;
serialized asset checks inspect the menu, training, rewards and splash
outputs. Final release verification binds passing evidence to the native
binary and all 17 delivered file hashes. Desktop Unity results are recorded
separately in local release reports when run.

Physical Quest 3 and real two-PC gameplay tests have not been run for this
release. Native fixtures and desktop checks do not establish those results.

The local delivery contains binary differences, instructions, own artwork
previews and applicable credits. Public source publication excludes game
binaries, extracted original assets, deltas, ZIPs and private validation
reports. Original artwork and synthesized audio retain their included
licenses; the retained Cloudcourt Bubble font is covered by Baloo2-OFL.txt.
