# Cloudcourt v28 — Extras and customization

This release fixes the spawned ball visual lifecycle, separates Indoor and Beach
block accounting, and extends Bomb to offline and online 2v2/4v4 through Extras.
It adds a camera-free same-avatar customization mirror, seven original hats
(cap, fedora, cowboy hat, motorcycle helmet, beanie, crown and visor), transparent
or outfit-colored arms, categorized Settings, independent Master/Music/Game/UI
volumes, a three-track original CC0 lofi playlist and the indoor flag startup emblem.

## Rules

An airborne opposing interception near/above the net is the supported block
proxy. Indoor blocks consume no team hit, Beach blocks consume one. Either
blocker may make the immediately following contact; repeated ordinary touches
remain illegal. Serve blocking remains a fault. This is a targeted VR adaptation,
not full simulation of all FIVB rules or body/hand penetration refereeing.

Primary references: FIVB Official Volleyball Rules 2025–2028,14.1/14.4/14.5:
https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf
FIVB Official Beach Volleyball Rules 2025–2028,14.2/14.4/14.5:
https://www.fivb.com/beach-volleyball/the-game/official-rules-of-the-games/
