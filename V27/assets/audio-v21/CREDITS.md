# Cloudcourt VR v21 audio

The serve whistle is an original procedural sound authored for Cloudcourt VR.
It is synthesized in serve_feedback_v21.c from two short triangle oscillators,
with a soft onset and release. No recording, music, sound library, brand audio,
or audio from another game is used. The clip is generated once and reused.

Existing replacement sounds and their redistribution licenses remain listed
in assets/audio-v18/CREDITS.md. No new external download is required.
